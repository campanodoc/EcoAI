Gianfranco Rubino

Researcher in AI literacy and ecoliteracy, trainer.

Profile

PhD in Learning Sciences and Digital Technologies (National Doctoral Programme, XXXVIII cycle, LUISS Guido Carli and University of Foggia). His research studies how artificial intelligence enters educational settings as a technical, social and environmental actor, and how to teach students and adults to read its functioning, biases and ecological impacts together.

Professional roles

ASNOR trainer: trains trainers in AI literacy for companies, schools and individuals.

Member of the ASNOR technical-scientific committee as an AI literacy and ecoliteracy expert.

The research line in three levels

Theory

Ecodigital Posthumanism, a theory in development on the co-constitution of ecologies, computational infrastructures and subjectivity. Current status: preprint.

Framework

The EcoAI Framework, a competence model in four dimensions (Systems Understanding, Ecological Understanding of AI, Identity Formation, Tool-Based Learning), aligned with GreenComp.

Instrument

The EcoAI Index, a questionnaire of five scales and 25 items, with preliminary validation on two surveys in secondary schools of southern Italy (403 and 198 questionnaires). The samples are school-based and not nationally representative.

Publications

Rubino, G. (2027). Eco-Digital Posthumanism: Rethinking Sociological Knowledge in the Digital Anthropocene. In Epistemic Shift in the Post-Digital Society: The Dilemma of Knowledge Production. McGraw-Hill. Forthcoming.
