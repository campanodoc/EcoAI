EcoAI Literacies

EcoAI Literacies are cross-cutting resources that support all four Framework domains. They are not an alternative taxonomy and do not coincide with the domains: they integrate the informational, digital, ecological, ethical and civic capacities needed to understand systems, analyse AI ecologically, form relational identities and conduct tool-mediated inquiry.

Versione italiana

Media & Information Literacy

Critical evaluation of sources, evidence, rhetoric, misinformation and responsible production.

Digital Competence

Information, communication, content creation, safety and problem solving.

Sustainability Competence

Values, systems thinking, possible futures and transformative action.

Futures Literacy

Scenarios, assumptions, anticipation and backcasting for present decisions.

Data Literacy

Provenance, quality, analysis, documentation, reproducibility and ethical data communication.

Algorithmic Literacy

Ranking, recommendation, platform power, bias, auditing and mitigation.

AI Literacy

Model capabilities and limits, training data, uncertainty, interpretability and responsible use.

Visualization Literacy

Charts and maps: scales, thresholds, encodings, uncertainty and accessibility.

Social and Platform Literacy

Critical, safe and collaborative participation in digital environments.

Planetary Systems

Flows, feedback, thresholds and interdependencies of Earth as a complex system.

Climate

Causes, impacts, mitigation, adaptation, carbon budgets and intergenerational equity.

Biodiversity & Ecosystems

Species–habitat relations, ecosystem services, citizen science and responsible monitoring.

Water

Water cycles, availability, quality, watersheds and transparent use of sensors and territorial data.

Energy

Sources, flows, efficiency, electricity demand and environmental trade-offs of digital and AI infrastructures.

Environmental Justice

Distribution of impacts, recognition, participation and protection of vulnerable communities.

Privacy & Data Protection

Minimisation, consent, anonymisation, legal bases and risks, especially for minors.

AI Ethics & Responsible Innovation

Transparency, fairness, accountability, traceability and impact assessment.

Civic & Deliberative Literacy

Public participation, pluralism, inclusion and transparent decision-making.
