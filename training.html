Training

Critical AI literacy for schools, companies and individuals, and training for trainers.

Who it is for

Schools (teachers and students), companies (teams and training managers), individuals and professionals, and those who train other trainers. Formats, durations and delivery modes are agreed with the client.

What participants learn

The approach derives from the EcoAI Framework and has four dimensions. Systems Understanding develops the reading of ecological and digital systems and their feedback loops. Ecological Understanding of AI foregrounds energy consumption, infrastructures and environmental justice of AI systems. Identity Formation concerns bias, fairness and algorithmic responsibility. Tool-Based Learning develops situated and critical use of AI tools, including generative ones.

How we work

The European reference is GreenComp, integrated with AI literacy models. Programmes use four-level rubrics (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, Autonomous-Critical) as teaching tools associated with the constructs, distinct from developmental stages or a linear progression.

Where useful, the EcoAI Index records a group profile before and after the programme. It returns a collective picture and should be read as monitoring, with the limits of a still preliminary validation.

Training for trainers

Programmes for those who teach AI literacy: conceptual framework, environmental and social impacts of AI, design of teaching units, criteria for assessing tools before adopting them.
