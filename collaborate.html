Collaborate

With institutions, training bodies, companies and research groups.

Assessment of AI systems in educational settings

Analysis of AI tools adopted or to be adopted in schools and training bodies, distinguishing three levels: direct environmental impact (energy, hardware, resources), media effects (attention economy, infrastructural power) and pedagogical implications (datafication, algorithmic governance, surveillance). AI is treated as an actor to be problematised and not as a neutral tool.

Measurement with the EcoAI Index

Administration of the Index to groups of students and reading of the collective profile across five dimensions (INFO, AATI, VAL, AGY, EPAS). The Index describes declared orientations of a group and does not assess individual competence.

Scientific consultancy and research projects

Participation in committees and working groups, co-design of programmes and studies, replication of the Index on independent and representative samples, integration of qualitative methods.

Status of validation

The validation of the Index is preliminary. The available samples are school-based and not nationally representative, the data are cross-sectional and self-reported, and replication on independent samples remains to be carried out. Research collaborations are the means to address this limit.
