EcoAI Framework: A Pedagogical Model for the Anthropocene

The EcoAI Framework proposes an interdisciplinary approach to integrate Ecoliteracy and Artificial Intelligence (AI) into civic education in secondary schools, shaping a new generation of eco-technological citizens.

Research Objectives

  • Explore students' perceptions of sustainability and AI.
  • Analyse the intersection between environmental and digital literacy.
  • Develop an integrated pedagogical model for civic education.

Research Questions

  • How do students perceive the impact of AI on environmental issues?
  • What is the level of ecoliteracy and environmental civic competences among young people?
  • How do digital media influence ecological citizenship?

Theoretical Framework

  • Eco-digital Posthumanism: Theoretical elaboration of the eco-digital posthumanist theory.
  • Ecomedia Literacy: Critical analysis of the ecological impact of media technologies.
  • Digital Civic Education and SDGs: National and international regulatory context of reference.

Scientific Foundations of the Framework

EcoAI is grounded in Eco-Digital Posthumanism and in research on ecoliteracy, digital media and artificial intelligence. The four domains are analytically distinct but interdependent: none is a prerequisite or terminal stage. EcoAI rubrics: function, limits and example

The rubrics serve a pedagogical and formative assessment function; they are not psychometric scales and are not equivalent to the EcoAI Index. Their levels describe qualitative differences in interpreting problems, representing relations, using tools and justifying choices.

Example: Systems Literacy and Mapping.

  1. Fragmented literacy: recognises ecological or digital elements but does not integrate them.
  2. Interconnected understanding: describes some relations among energy, matter, information, technology and environment.
  3. Mature systems literacy: maps components, processes, flows, criticalities and delays with appropriate tools.
  4. Advanced eco-digital literacy: integrates data infrastructures, energy, algorithmic governance, digital materiality and planetary limits.

Positioning in relation to international frameworks

EcoAI brings together sustainability, digital infrastructures, artificial intelligence, information practices, agency and subject positioning. GreenComp, DigComp and educational AI-literacy frameworks are comparative references with different functions: the correspondence is conceptual and pedagogical, not direct derivation or evidence of validation.

GreenComp: European framework of competences for sustainability

GreenComp organises sustainability competence into four interconnected areas: Embodying sustainability values, Embracing complexity in sustainability, Envisioning sustainable futures and Acting for sustainability. EcoAI shares its attention to values, complexity, futures and action, while taking the relations among ecological systems, digital infrastructures and AI as its specific object.

AI literacy, DigComp, UNESCO and OECD

DigComp provides a reference for digital competence; UNESCO and OECD–European Commission frameworks directly address AI-related competences; AI-literacy research helps distinguish understanding, use, critical evaluation and responsibility. EcoAI uses them for functional positioning without presenting them as direct sources of its taxonomy or as validation of its rubrics and EcoAI Index.

EcoAI's specificity lies in how these problems are configured together, not in a claim to replace or surpass existing frameworks.

Integration of SDGs in the European and Italian context

The SDGs provide pedagogical references for connecting EcoAI domains to concrete problems; they do not validate the Framework. Activities may, for example, examine electricity demand, energy mix, carbon footprint, water use and the unequal distribution of AI's ecological costs, or support global-citizenship and collective-action practices addressing transnational environmental problems.

Alignment with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

The EcoAI framework directly contributes to numerous United Nations Sustainable Development Goals, promoting education for a fair and sustainable future.
